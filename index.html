About CRÈME

Only croissants. Done slowly.

CRÈME is a fictional, croissant-only bakery imagined for this ordering demo. Every pastry starts from the same cultured-butter dough, rested overnight and folded by hand until it bakes into fine, shattering layers.

We keep the menu short on purpose: one classic, seven fillings, and a counter that empties by evening.
